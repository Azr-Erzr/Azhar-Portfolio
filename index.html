WasFirst

Prove you were there first.

Upload an old screenshot, receipt, ticket stub, or photo — WasFirst's AI pipeline identifies the clue, estimates when it was captured, and scores how early you were to an artist, a meme, a product. The result becomes a collectible share card, with 23 card designs across free and premium tiers.

Why it's interesting: this isn't a demo — it's an operated business. Stripe checkout and webhooks, gift codes, admin tooling, account deletion flows, and 19 Supabase Edge Functions behind the scenes. The AI dating engine has its own evaluation harness: golden datasets, synthetic cases, and multi-lane promptfoo evals that gate changes before they ship.

Sel-Fi

A marketplace for seller-financed housing.

Sel-Fi connects property sellers and buyers for direct deals — seller-financed, rent-to-own, and private sales. Sellers keep their commission and earn interest; buyers get flexible paths to ownership outside the traditional mortgage gate. Browsable listings, buyer and investor profiles, and deal-structure detail pages.

Why it's interesting: it's domain knowledge turned into product. Creative financing is a real but underserved corner of housing — my urban planning and real estate finance background shaped the deal structures, the listing data model, and the buyer-profile matching idea.

azr-erzr.github.io
Zero-infrastructure · Open source

OIART Rental Finder

A full search-and-ranking app that costs $0 to run.

A live rental-hunting tool where a Google Sheet is the database, GitHub Pages is the host, and a small Apps Script endpoint handles write-back. Every listing is scored 0–100 by an honesty-first engine: graduated points for what a listing actually states, unknowns excluded from the math instead of fake-penalized, and missing must-haves capping the score so a mystery listing can never look like a 95.

Why it's interesting: it's maximum product from minimum infrastructure — map with drive-time anchors, team workflow tracking, scam flags, CSV fallback — with no server and no hosting bill. I also wrote a public step-by-step guide so anyone can rebuild it for any search: cars, houses, jobs.

  • Vanilla JS
  • Google Sheets API
  • Apps Script
  • Leaflet
  • GitHub Pages
